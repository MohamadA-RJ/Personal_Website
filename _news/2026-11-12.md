## Awarded NWO Open Science NL Funding

Delighted to share that our project has been awarded funding through the **[FAIR Research Outputs programme](https://www.openscience.nl/en/researchprogrammes/fair-research-outputs)**, run by **[Open Science NL](https://www.openscience.nl/en)** in partnership with **[NWO](https://www.nwo.nl/en)**. I am proud to be the **main applicant and project leader**, together with co-applicant Prof. Clemens Dransfeld.

---

## About the Project

- **Project proposal title:** *3DMicroStructLab: Open Interactive Workflows for Realistic and Reproducible 3D Composite Microstructures*
- **Public project title:** *Zooming In, Opening Up: Transparent Recreation of the Micro-world of Composites*

Carbon-fibre composites help make aircraft and wind turbines lighter, but their performance depends on the microscopic arrangement of their fibres. Researchers use different methods to analyse and recreate these arrangements, which can make results difficult to compare and reproduce.

**3DMicroStructLab** will provide an **open, interactive framework** that researchers can calibrate to their own measurements. Real examples from high-resolution X-ray scans of carbon-fibre composites will connect measured structures to realistic digital models. By making this workflow accessible and reproducible, the project will help researchers compare methods and build on one another's work.
