## Master's Thesis Opportunities

I am offering **three master's thesis projects** with Clemens Dransfeld at [PAAM Lab](https://www.paam-lab.org/), TU Delft, exploring new approaches to modelling and generating 3D fiber-reinforced composite microstructures:

- **Deep generative models:** Learn microstructure patterns and continuity across depth directly from CT image stacks using diffusion models or conditional variational autoencoders.
- **Neighborhood-aware stochastic generation:** Extend our statistical framework to preserve coherent fiber groups and persistent spatial neighborhoods.
- **Blender-based compaction simulations:** Investigate how nip-roller processing parameters influence fiber rearrangement and misalignment through soft-body simulations.

Explore the **[full proposals on the PAAM Lab opportunities page and many more interesting topics](https://www.paam-lab.org/opportunities/)**.
